# QUSM Quota System — Staff Guide

## How to submit your quota
Quota submissions are now made through the Staff Team Discord server. You do not need to use the old website quota leaderboard or a separate website quota form.

### Step 1 — Use the Discord command
Run:

`/quota-submit`

You will be asked for:
- **Minutes** — enter the number of minutes you completed.
- **Proof** — attach the proof image directly to the command.
- **Notes** — optional; use this if Logistics needs extra context.

### Step 2 — Submit once
After submitting, the bot confirms that your quota is waiting for Logistics review. Do not repeatedly submit the same quota while it is pending.

### Step 3 — Wait for Logistics
Logistics will review the proof.

- **Approved:** the minutes are automatically added to your staff record in the Google Staff Database.
- **Rejected:** you will receive a Discord DM containing the reason for rejection. Read the reason and correct the issue before submitting again when appropriate.

## Leaderboard
The live quota leaderboard is now available in Discord with:

`/quota-leaderboard`

The leaderboard is restricted to members with the **Staff Team server Staff role**. The old website Staff role is no longer used for leaderboard access.

## Proof requirements
- Attach the proof image directly to `/quota-submit`.
- Make sure the proof is clear and actually supports the minutes claimed.
- Use notes when additional explanation is useful.
- Do not submit fabricated, duplicate, or unrelated proof.

## What happens after approval?
The system adds the approved minutes to your existing quota. You do not need to ask Logistics to update the spreadsheet manually.

An approval log is also recorded by the bot for staff-management purposes.

## Future quota logging
A dedicated logging feature will be added in a future update. It is planned to work more like a structured ticket/logging system, so quota submissions and their history can be followed more cleanly. For now, Discord submission, Logistics review, approval/rejection, DM notifications, and the Google Staff Database remain the official quota workflow.

## Need help?
If a command is missing, the proof cannot be attached, or you do not receive the expected result, contact Staff/Logistics rather than creating multiple duplicate submissions.
