# QUSM Quota System — Staff Guide

## How to submit quota
All Staff quota submissions are handled through the Staff Team Discord server.

Use:
`/quota-submit`

The command requires:
- **Minutes** — the amount of quota you completed.
- **Proof** — attach the required proof image directly to the command.
- **Notes** — optional additional information for Logistics.

After submission, your request goes to the Logistics review channel. Do not assume the minutes are counted until Logistics approves the request.

## If your quota is rejected
If Logistics rejects your submission, you will receive a Discord DM containing the rejection reason. The original review message is marked **Rejected**, and the rejection is recorded in the same quota log channel used for approvals. Rejected quota is **not** added to your Google Staff Database total.

Read the reason and correct the issue before submitting again where appropriate.

## Leaderboard
Use:
`/quota-leaderboard`

The leaderboard is available to members with the Staff Team server staff role. It reads the live quota data from the QUSM Staff Database.

## Logging requirement
Staff are expected to log their required activity in **both of the designated places/documents** used by the current Staff process. The quota submission through Discord is the official quota record; continue following any separate staff logging/document requirement issued by leadership.

Do not put proof or notes into the Google Staff Database yourself. The approval workflow handles the quota update.

## Future logging system
A more complete logging feature will be added later, with a workflow similar to tickets/log cases. It will make it easier to record and track different staff actions in one place.

For now, that feature is **not the general logging system**. The active automated logging workflow is specifically for **quota logging** and its submission, review, approval/rejection, and database record.

## Quick reference
1. Complete your quota activity.
2. Use `/quota-submit`.
3. Enter the correct minutes.
4. Attach clear proof.
5. Add notes if needed.
6. Wait for Logistics review.
7. If approved, the minutes are added to your database total and an approval record is logged.
8. If rejected, check your DM for the reason; the rejection is logged and no minutes are added.
